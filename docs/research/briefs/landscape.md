## 1. The other payments / stablecoin-first chains

### Plasma (XPL) — cautionary tale turned partial recovery

- Bitcoin-anchored, EVM-compatible L1 purpose-built around USDT; mainnet late September 2025 with a protocol-level paymaster sponsoring gas for simple USDT transfers (zero-fee sends, no XPL needed) ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/372300/stablecoin-layer-1-plasma-goes-live-introducing-xpl-token-and-defi-integrations), [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11802920-what-is-plasma-xpl-stablecoin-l1-in-2026)).
- The trajectory is the category's key data point: post-launch TVL peaked around $5.6–6.3B, then collapsed — stablecoin supply fell from $6.3B to ~$1.8B, daily active addresses down ~90%, transactions down ~75%, and XPL lost >85% from its high by late 2025 ([The Merkle](https://themerkle.com/plasmas-shine-fades-as-tvl-users-and-token-value-collapse/), [99Bitcoins](https://99bitcoins.com/news/altcoins/plasma-xpl-crypto-loses-85-of-its-value-in-six-weeks-what-happened/), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/10/30/plasma-s-xpl-token-crashes-80-as-hype-fades-amid-woeful-debut)). Most stablecoins on the chain sat in lending pools rather than moving as payments ([CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/insights/analytics/stablecoin-chain-prospects)).
- Partial rebound in Q2 2026 driven by Tether's tether.wallet launch (April 14, 2026), which named Plasma one of four "first-class" networks alongside Ethereum, Polygon and Arbitrum; TVL recrossed ~$2B ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/why-plasma-s-tvl-surged-to-2-billion-tether-integration-and-the-rise-of-the-stablecoin-native-l1)). TVL figures conflict across sources; treat point-in-time numbers with caution.

### Stable (the "USDT chain")

- Bitfinex-backed L1 using USDT as the native gas token. $28M seed led by Bitfinex and Hack VC with Paolo Ardoino advising ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/364952/tether-focused-layer-1-stable-raises-28-million-seed-round-to-boost-usdt-adoption)); mainnet + STABLE token launched December 8, 2025 after a >$2B pre-deposit campaign from 24,000+ wallets ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/381688/stable-launches-mainnet-native-token-foundation)).
- Feb 4, 2026 v1.2.0 upgrade made USDT0 (LayerZero OFT variant) the native gas token with API-managed gas waivers; roadmap targets a DAG-based consensus ("Autobahn") and StableVM++ at 10k+ TPS by Q2 2026, plus guaranteed blockspace for institutional partners ([BingX](https://bingx.com/en/learn/article/what-is-stable-stable-usdt-stablechain-how-does-it-work)). Launched a USDT institutional yield product in 2026 ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/402582/tether-focused-chain-stable-launches-usdt-institutional-yield-product)).
- Notable: Tether's ecosystem is now running *two* competing house chains (Plasma and Stable), splitting its own distribution.

### Codex — the institutional/enterprise stablecoin L2

- Stablecoin-only Ethereum L2 (OP-stack per L2BEAT); $15.8M seed led by Dragonfly with Coinbase and Circle participating ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/04/04/codex-seed-round-dragonfly-coinbase-circle-15-8-million/), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/349636/codex-the-startup-behind-an-enterprise-focused-blockchain-for-stablecoins-raises-15-8-million-in-dragonfly-led-seed-round)). Mainnet June 24, 2025 with native USDC mint/burn (no bridge in the path) ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/stablecoin-blockchain-codex-launches-mainnet-with-usdc-support)).
- Positioning is institutional FX and B2B settlement (notably Africa corridors), not retail remittance — a deliberately different segment than Plasma/Stable/Tron ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/08/27/codex-blockchain-settlement-layer-for-africa/)). Integrated by Elliptic for compliance analytics ([Elliptic](https://www.elliptic.co/media-center/elliptic-marks-milestone-50th-blockchain-integration-with-codex)) and is a CCTP v2 chain ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11813797-circle-cctp-v2-native-usdc-across-13-chains)).

### Sui and Aptos — general L1s pivoting hard to payments

- Sui: gasless stablecoin transfers went live May 20, 2026 with Fireblocks support ([ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/new-payments-push-sui-gasless-stablecoin-transfers-enter-the-spotlight/69548870)), and Sui announced **privacy-by-default (encrypted) stablecoin transactions**, deployed on testnet with mainnet "very soon" ([Crypto Economy](https://crypto-economy.com/sui-introduces-encrypted-stablecoin-payments-during-a-new-privacy-push/)).
- Aptos: ecosystem stablecoin market cap passed $2B in June 2026; core partner in the **Open USD** consortium (launched June 30, 2026 — Mastercard, Visa, Stripe, BlackRock, Coinbase among 140+ firms) ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/crypto/markets/aptos-visa-and-blackrock-among-140-firms-launching-new-stablecoin), [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/6a451eb1918d3b308fdbd402/)); also building a regulated Middle East–Africa payments corridor with HashKey MENA and Daya ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/aptos-powers-stablecoin-payment-corridor-221049347.html)). Open USD is arguably the biggest mid-2026 structural event: distribution incumbents forming a consortium stablecoin rather than aligning to one chain ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/coinbase-helped-build-usdc-why-its-now-backing-the-stablecoin-trying-to-replace-it-open-usd/)).

### Solana — the incumbent payments winner by volume

- Visa launched USDC settlement on Solana for US merchant acquirers; the pilot passed $3.5B annualized volume ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/382721/visa-launches-stablecoin-settlement-us-circle-usdc-solana), [Visa](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.19881.html)). Worldpay settles in USDG on Solana; ~57% of USDG is issued there; Western Union is issuing a stablecoin on Solana; BVNK powers stablecoin infrastructure for Visa Direct (Jan 2026) ([Messari](https://messari.io/report/state-of-solana-payments)).
- Solana's pitch is credible neutrality + existing liquidity + fees/finality already good enough — an argument against payment-specific chains.

### Base / Coinbase — distribution-led stablecoin strategy

- Coinbase Payments (stablecoin checkout, Shopify protocol on Base), Nium partnership for USDC payouts across 190+ countries, and a trust charter clearing a key regulatory hurdle (April 2026) ([American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/coinbase-partners-with-nium-to-boost-stablecoins), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/02/coinbase-clears-key-regulatory-hurdle-in-bid-to-bolster-its-stablecoin-business.html)). Coinbase B2B stablecoin volume grew from <$100M/mo (2023) to ~$3B/mo (mid-2025); stablecoin revenue was 22% of total in Q2 2025 ([AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/coinbase-2026-flow-bet-analyzing-stablecoin-payment-engine-2604/)). Coinbase also joined Open USD despite co-founding USDC — hedging on distribution ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/coinbase-helped-build-usdc-why-its-now-backing-the-stablecoin-trying-to-replace-it-open-usd/)).

### Canton Network — the regulated-asset heavyweight

- DTCC + Digital Asset partnership (Dec 2025) to tokenize DTC-custodied U.S. Treasuries on Canton: MVP in controlled production 1H 2026, broader rollout 2H 2026 ([canton.network](https://www.canton.network/canton-network-press-releases/dtcc-and-digital-asset-partner-to-tokenize-dtc-custodied-u-s-treasury-securities-on-the-canton-network)).
- Kinexys plans to bring JPM Coin (JPMD) natively onto Canton in a phased 2026 rollout; HSBC completed a tokenized-deposit pilot on Canton in April 2026; Broadridge's DLR processes **$8T+/month in repo** on Canton infrastructure ([Genfinity](https://genfinity.io/2026/01/29/canton-network-institutional-blockchain-overview/), [canton.network](https://www.canton.network/)). Canton's differentiators are sub-transaction privacy and permissioned interoperability — the closest analogue to a "regulated-asset chain" actually winning institutional volume.

### Fnality / Partior / Kinexys — wholesale bank rails

- Fnality: settlement in central bank money on-chain (vs. commercial bank money for Kinexys/Partior); raised $136M Series C to expand, including a US dollar system ([AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/fnality-blockchain-breakthrough-136m-replace-swift-instant-risk-free-payments-2509/), [Fnality](https://fnality.com/news/global-regulation-of-stablecoins-and-tokenized-deposits-remains-fragmented-in-2026)).
- Kinexys (JPMorgan): deposit tokens (JPMD), Synchronized Settlements; May 2026 first cross-border, cross-bank redemption of tokenized U.S. Treasuries with Ondo, Mastercard and Ripple ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ondo-kinexys-by-jp-morgan-mastercard-and-ripple-complete-first-cross-border-cross-bank-redemption-of-tokenized-us-treasuries-302764324.html)); DBS + Kinexys framework for interbank tokenized deposit transfers across multiple blockchains ([DBS](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_and_Kinexys_by_JP_Morgan_to_develop_framework_for_interbank_tokenised_deposit_transfers_across_multiple_blockchains)); cross-chain settlement tests with Chainlink/Ondo ([JPMorgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/kinexys-chainlink-ondo-tokenized-asset-test)).
- Partior (JPM/DBS/Temasek): multi-bank interbank clearing network, still live but lower public velocity than Canton/Kinexys in 2026 coverage ([StablecoinAtlas](https://stablecoinatlas.com/rail/wholesale/)).

### Hyperliquid — USDH sunset

- Native Markets' USDH (reserves via Stripe's Bridge) launched, then in May 2026 **Coinbase acquired the USDH brand assets and USDH is being sunset in favor of USDC**, with Coinbase as Hyperliquid's official USDC treasury deployer; conversions supported until July 17, 2026 ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/401233/coinbase-hyperliquid-official-deployer-usdc), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/341087/hyperliquid-aligned-usdh-native-markets-stablecoin-live-hype-falls)). HIP-3 (permissionless builder-deployed perp markets) is doing ~$3B/day and continues with existing USDH markets at deployer discretion ([usdh.com](https://usdh.com/migration)). Lesson: even a chain with massive organic volume found issuing its own stablecoin harder than renting USDC's distribution.

## 2. Table-stakes features that emerged in 2025–2026

Every credible entrant now ships, or is expected to ship:

1. **Stablecoin-as-gas / gasless UX** — Plasma's paymaster, Stable's USDT gas + API gas waivers, Sui's gasless transfers, Tempo's stablecoin fees. Requiring users to hold a native token to move dollars is disqualifying ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11802920-what-is-plasma-xpl-stablecoin-l1-in-2026), [BingX](https://bingx.com/en/learn/article/what-is-stable-stable-usdt-stablechain-how-does-it-work)).
2. **Sub-second (or near-instant) finality claims** — universal marketing across Plasma (PlasmaBFT), Stable, Tempo, Arc, Sui/Aptos; Solana's speed is the benchmark competitors cite ([Messari](https://messari.io/report/state-of-solana-payments)).
3. **Confidential-but-auditable transfers** — Solana Token-2022 Confidential Balances (ZK-shielded amounts with auditor keys) ([Helius](https://www.helius.dev/blog/confidential-balances), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/350076/solana-developers-launch-new-confidential-balances-token-extensions-to-improve-onchain-privacy)); Sui privacy-by-default stablecoin transactions ([Crypto Economy](https://crypto-economy.com/sui-introduces-encrypted-stablecoin-payments-during-a-new-privacy-push/)); Canton's sub-transaction privacy. Pattern: privacy from the public, visibility for issuer/regulator ([TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/reports-and-whitepapers/on-chain-privacy-and-financial-compliance)).
4. **Compliance hooks** — allowlists/freeze/clawback at the token layer (Token-2022 extensions), chain-level analytics integrations (Elliptic on Codex), travel-rule messaging (Notabene/TRISA/Veriscope), and EU TFR zero-threshold CASP-to-CASP data requirements ([eco.com travel rule guide](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15210554-travel-rule-for-stablecoins-2026-compliance-guide), [InnReg](https://www.innreg.com/blog/crypto-travel-rule-guide)).
5. **Native issuer mint/burn + fiat on/off ramps at launch** — Codex launched with native USDC mint/burn; ramps via Coinbase/Nium/BVNK-class partners are expected day one ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/stablecoin-blockchain-codex-launches-mainnet-with-usdc-support), [Grafa](https://grafa.com/en/news/crypto/coinbase-usdc-payments-nium-global)).
6. **Card-network integrations and named launch partners** — Visa USDC settlement (Solana + Ethereum/Stellar/Avalanche), Worldpay/USDG, Western Union, Visa Direct via BVNK; Open USD launched with Visa + Mastercard + Stripe + BlackRock attached. A launch without named payment-industry partners is now read as a launch without distribution ([Visa](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.19881.html), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/crypto/markets/aptos-visa-and-blackrock-among-140-firms-launching-new-stablecoin)).

## 3. Post-quantum cryptography in blockchains, 2026

**Regulatory/migration pressure is real and dated.** NIST FIPS 203/204/205 finalized Aug 2024 (HQC backup KEM selected Mar 2025, finalization ~2026–27) ([NIST](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography)). NSA CNSA 2.0: new national security system acquisitions must be PQ-compliant from Jan 1, 2027, with full migration milestones through 2030–2035 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-15-billion-post-quantum-migration-nist-standards-are-final-nsa-deadlines-are-set-and-enterprise-cybersecurity-is-about-to-be-rebuilt-from-the-ground-up-302730679.html), [gopher.security](https://www.gopher.security/news/nist-2026-post-quantum-cryptography-migration-guide)).

**Who has what:**

- **Ethereum** — PQ became a top strategic priority in Jan 2026; Vitalik published a four-area PQ roadmap (Feb 2026); the EF launched pq.ethereum.org with 10+ client teams (Mar 2026). Plan: replace BLS12-381 validator keys with **leanXMSS** hash-based signatures (~3KB each) aggregated via a SNARK/leanVM; **EIP-8141** (opt-in per-account quantum-safe checks) considered for the Hegota fork in 2H 2026 ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/02/26/vitalik-buterin-unveils-ethereum-roadmap-to-counter-quantum-computing-threat), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/03/25/ethereum-foundation-prepares-for-quantum-threat-with-new-cryptography-roadmap), [pq.ethereum.org](https://pq.ethereum.org/), [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/ethereum-researchers-lay-out-post-quantum-key-registry-as-first-concrete-migration-step)). All roadmap, none shipped to mainnet consensus yet.
- **Bitcoin** — **BIP-360** (quantum-resistant address type, P2MR) merged into the BIPs repo Feb 11, 2026; migration remains a long-horizon debate ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/bitcoin-is-going-quantum-proof-inside-bip-360-and-the-migration/)).
- **Algorand** — first PQ transaction using Falcon signatures (2025), but only at the state-proof layer; account keys remain Ed25519 ([QuantumShield wiki](https://quantumwalletcheck.com/wiki/quantum-resistant-blockchains)).
- **Solana** — Winternitz vault (hash-based, opt-in) proposed late 2025; <300 accounts using it as of April 2026 ([QuantumShield wiki](https://quantumwalletcheck.com/wiki/quantum-resistant-blockchains)).
- **QRL** — fully hash-based (XMSS) since genesis; the only chain PQ end-to-end, but with negligible payments relevance ([Webopedia](https://www.webopedia.com/crypto/learn/post-quantum-crypto-projects/), [Tangem](https://tangem.com/en/blog/post/quantum-resistant-crypto/)).
- **Arc (Circle)** — announced Apr 2026 that mainnet will include a PQ signature scheme for **optional quantum-resistant wallets** ([Arc blog](https://www.arc.io/blog/arcs-quantum-resistant-design-and-roadmap-why-it-matters)) — wallet-level opt-in, not the chain's primary signing surface.

**Assessment:** No payments/stablecoin/regulated-asset chain is shipping NIST-standardized PQ signatures (ML-DSA/ML-KEM) as its primary signing surface in 2026. Tempo, Arc, Plasma, Stable, Codex, Canton and the bank rails all launched on classical crypto. PQ is a live *narrative* (Google's Mar 2026 estimate of a ~9-minute quantum key crack amplified it — [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/quantum-computers-bitcoin-2026-q-day)) and a genuine open differentiator, especially for institutions facing CNSA 2.0 procurement timelines — but it is not yet table stakes, and no competitor in the payments category has it in production.

## 4. Cross-chain settlement standards — what's winning

- **CCTP v2 (Circle burn-and-mint)** is the default for native USDC/EURC across 13+ chains (incl. Solana, Sui, Aptos, Base, Codex) — canonical, no wrapped assets, but issuer-only ([Circle](https://www.circle.com/cross-chain-transfer-protocol), [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11813797-circle-cctp-v2-native-usdc-across-13-chains)).
- **LayerZero OFT** is the default for everything else: USDT0 moved **$70B+ cross-chain in its first 12 months**, and Tether made a strategic investment in LayerZero Labs (Feb 10, 2026) ([Tether](https://tether.io/news/tether-announces-strategic-investment-in-layerzero-labs-creator-of-the-interoperability-infrastructure-used-by-usdt0/), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/389222/tether-makes-strategic-investment-into-layerzero-labs-creator-of-tech-used-for-usdt0), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/02/10/tether-invests-in-layerzero-labs-as-it-doubles-down-on-cross-chain-tech-agentic-finance)). PYUSD also uses OFT.
- In practice they're **complementary, composed by aggregators** (LI.FI, Squid, deBridge): CCTP for the USDC leg, OFT/Wormhole NTT for others; chain abstraction is being delivered by these orchestration layers rather than by any single standard ([stablecoininsider.org](https://stablecoininsider.org/cross-chain-stablecoin-bridges-2026/), [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14747318-stablecoin-infrastructure-providers-2026)).
- Bank-side interop is forming separately: DBS–Kinexys multi-chain tokenized-deposit framework, Kinexys–Chainlink–Ondo cross-chain settlement tests ([DBS](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_and_Kinexys_by_JP_Morgan_to_develop_framework_for_interbank_tokenised_deposit_transfers_across_multiple_blockchains), [JPMorgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/kinexys-chainlink-ondo-tokenized-asset-test)).

## 5. Regulatory context shaping chain design

- **GENIUS Act (US, enacted July 18, 2025):** implementation is in full swing at the July 18, 2026 statutory rulemaking deadline. FinCEN/OFAC proposed AML + sanctions rules (April 8, 2026) treating permitted payment stablecoin issuers as BSA financial institutions; OCC, FDIC, NCUA proposals are out; only the Fed lags; effective date ~2027 ([Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0435), [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/10/2026-06963/permitted-payment-stablecoin-issuer-anti-money-launderingcountering-the-financing-of-terrorism), [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/04/genius-act-implementation-key-proposals-and-what-comes-next), [Chapman tracker](https://www.chapman.com/publication-genius-act-rulemaking-tracker)). Design consequence: issuer-level freeze/seize capability, sanctions screening, and SAR-capable monitoring are becoming mandatory plumbing, pushing chains toward token-layer compliance hooks.
- **MiCA (EU):** transitional period ends July 1, 2026 — only MiCA-authorized EMTs (USDC, EURC, EURI, EURCV, etc.) remain in EEA regulated markets; EMT issuers need credit-institution or e-money authorization; from March 2026 EMT custody/transfer may need both MiCA and PSD2 licensing ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica), [Sumsub](https://sumsub.com/blog/crypto-regulations-in-the-european-union-markets-in-crypto-assets-mica/), [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14796308-mica-compliant-stablecoins-list)). EU TFR applies a **zero-value threshold** for CASP-to-CASP travel-rule data ([InnReg](https://www.innreg.com/blog/crypto-travel-rule-guide)). USDT's non-MiCA status is a structural reason Tether is building its own chains and USDT0.
- Wholesale/tokenized-deposit regulation remains fragmented globally, which favors permissioned networks (Canton, Kinexys, Fnality) for bank money ([Fnality](https://fnality.com/news/global-regulation-of-stablecoins-and-tokenized-deposits-remains-fragmented-in-2026)).

## 6. What makes these chains succeed or stall

- **Distribution beats technology.** Plasma's collapse-then-recovery tracked one variable: whether Tether's wallet pointed users at it ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/why-plasma-s-tvl-surged-to-2-billion-tether-integration-and-the-rise-of-the-stablecoin-native-l1)). Solana wins payments via Visa/Worldpay/Western Union integrations, not TPS ([Messari](https://messari.io/report/state-of-solana-payments)). Open USD is explicitly a distribution war — "the stablecoin war moved from market cap to distribution" ([CryptoDaily](https://cryptodaily.co.uk/2026/07/circle-vs-open-usd-distribution-war)).
- **Mercenary TVL ≠ payments.** Incentivized deposits parked in lending pools evaporate; Plasma's DAU fell 90% once airdrop farming ended ([CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/insights/analytics/stablecoin-chain-prospects), [AMBCrypto](https://ambcrypto.com/plasma-xpl-loses-steam-after-airdrop-surge-is-the-new-chain-buzz-over/)).
- **Issuer alignment is decisive.** Hyperliquid's USDH died within months despite enormous native volume — Coinbase/Circle's USDC distribution won ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/401233/coinbase-hyperliquid-official-deployer-usdc)).
- **Wallet and ramp integration is the practical bottleneck** — multi-chain stablecoin support, gas abstraction, and reconciliation are where products fail ([PrimeFin Labs](https://primefinlabs.com/stablecoin-wallet-infrastructure/)); "big partner logos do not equal deep books on day one" ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14747318-stablecoin-infrastructure-providers-2026)).
- **For regulated-asset chains, anchor institutions are the moat**: Canton's DTCC/Broadridge/HSBC/Kinexys pipeline is worth more than any public-chain TVL number ([canton.network](https://www.canton.network/canton-network-press-releases/dtcc-and-digital-asset-partner-to-tokenize-dtc-custodied-u-s-treasury-securities-on-the-canton-network)).
