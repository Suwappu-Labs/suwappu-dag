## 1. Current status (mid-2026)

**Robinhood Chain's public mainnet is live.** It launched **July 1, 2026**, announced at Robinhood's "The World Is Flat" keynote in London, alongside new 24/7 Stock Tokens, DeFi products, and agentic trading ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/01/robinhood-rolls-out-public-blockchain-as-it-expands-deeper-into-crypto); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)).

Timeline:

- **Jun 30, 2025** — First-generation "stock tokens" launched for EU/EEA users: 200+ US stock and ETF tokens issued on **Arbitrum One** (not yet Robinhood's own chain), with a stated plan to later migrate to a Robinhood L2 built on the Orbit stack ([Arbitrum announcement](https://x.com/arbitrum/status/1939710512205582609); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/07/robinhood-stock-tokens-face-scrutiny-in-the-eu-after-openai-warning.html)).
- **Feb 10, 2026** — Robinhood Chain **public testnet** launched, with docs, faucet (test ETH plus test tokens for TSLA, AMZN, PLTR, NFLX, AMD), block explorer, and Arbitrum Bridge integration ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-chain-launches-public-testnet/); [Arbitrum blog](https://blog.arbitrum.io/robinhood-chain-testnet/)). The testnet reportedly processed ~4 million transactions in its first week ([Yahoo Finance/CoinDesk](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/robinhood-arbitrum-l2-chain-launches-124715466.html)).
- **Jul 1, 2026** — Mainnet launch; new Stock Tokens available to eligible users in **120+ countries** via the self-custodial Robinhood Wallet ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/)).

**EU rollout status:** The EU remains the flagship market. The original offering is now branded "**Classic Stock Tokens**" — derivative contracts with Robinhood Europe, UAB (Bank of Lithuania-regulated), trading 24/5, zero commission plus 0.1% FX fee ([Robinhood EU support](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/about-stock-tokens/); [FAQ](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/stock-tokens-faq/)). The new on-chain Stock Tokens (24/7, self-custodied) launched globally on mainnet, **excluding the US, Canada, UK, Switzerland, UAE, and sanctioned jurisdictions** ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)). Also announced: EU expansion of 24/7 perpetual futures to commodities, ETFs, and FX (up to 10x leverage: GOLD, SILVER, QQQ, EUR/USD, WTI, Brent) ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/)).

## 2. Technical architecture

- **Stack:** Ethereum Layer 2 built with the **Arbitrum Orbit / Nitro** stack (Robinhood docs call it an "Arbitrum Dedicated Blockchain"), settling to Ethereum and inheriting its security ([docs.robinhood.com/chain](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/); [Arbitrum blog](https://blog.arbitrum.io/robinhood-chain-testnet/)).
- **EVM compatibility:** Fully EVM-compatible; Solidity/Vyper contracts deploy unmodified with standard tooling (Hardhat, Foundry, ethers.js) ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/)).
- **Gas token:** **ETH** is the native gas token ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/)).
- **Sequencer:** **First-come-first-served (FCFS) sequencing** — ordering strictly by arrival time at the sequencer, which limits fee-based reordering/MEV auctions ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/)). Reporting describes a **single, Robinhood-operated centralized sequencer hosted on AWS with multisig control**; no public decentralization roadmap has been detailed ([CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/02/robinhood-chain-claims-4x-solana-speed-with-100ms-blocks-but-do-we-really-need-ultra-fast-blockchains/)). Galaxy Digital notes the model mirrors Coinbase's Base: Robinhood controls the sequencer and captures its fees ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/robinhood-tokenization-nyse-liquidity-shift)).
- **Performance:** ~**100 ms block times** with near-instant soft confirmations; marketing framed this as "4x Solana speed" ([CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/02/robinhood-chain-claims-4x-solana-speed-with-100ms-blocks-but-do-we-really-need-ultra-fast-blockchains/)).
- **Data availability mode:** Not specified in public docs (whether classic rollup DA to Ethereum or AnyTrust committee) — **unconfirmed**.
- **Openness/bridging:** Described as **permissionless** — anyone can deploy contracts. LayerZero provides omnichain messaging/bridging; Chainlink provides price feeds and cross-chain oracle infrastructure ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/); [PR Newswire — Chainlink](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/robinhood-chain-launches-and-adopts-chainlink-to-unlock-access-to-the-onchain-economy-for-millions-of-users-302816242.html)).

## 3. Tokenized assets: stock token design

Two generations coexist:

**Classic Stock Tokens (EU, 2025-era):**

- **Derivative contracts** between the user and Robinhood Europe, UAB, tracking US stocks/ETFs; no ownership rights in the underlying ([Robinhood EU](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/about-stock-tokens/)).
- Underlying assets "held securely by a US-licensed institution" (hedging structure) ([Robinhood EU](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/about-stock-tokens/)).
- **24/5 trading** (Mon 02:00 CET – Sat 02:00 CET, closed on 10 US market holidays); dividends **passed through in cash**; **not transferable** to external wallets ([Robinhood EU](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/about-stock-tokens/)).

**New Stock Tokens (Robinhood Chain, July 2026):**

- **Tokenized debt securities** issued by **Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited (RHJ)** — economic exposure to US shares/ETFs, but **no legal or beneficial rights** in the underlying stocks (no voting, no shareholder rights) ([docs — Stock Tokens](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)). Investors are directed to the Base Prospectus and Final Terms for restrictions ([eco.com explainer](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15254023-tokenized-equities-2026-backed-dinari-robinhood)).
- **Standard ERC-20s, self-custodied** in Robinhood Wallet or any wallet — holdable, transferable, and composable in DeFi (lending pools, trading collateral) ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/); [Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/)). This fulfills the self-custody/transferability promise the Classic tokens lacked.
- **24/7 trading** on-chain via DEXs (Uniswap, Rialto, Lighter, Arcus, 1inch) ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/)).
- **Dividends/corporate actions:** handled by an **on-chain multiplier** adjusting the shares-per-token ratio (raw balance stays static until redemption), exposed via a `uiMultiplier()` function under the **ERC-8056** standard; Chainlink price feeds incorporate the multiplier ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/)).
- **Mint/redeem:** only **Authorized Participants** (initially "BBVI") can mint/redeem directly with RHJ after KYB onboarding ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/)).
- **Custody:** BitGo provides day-one institutional custody support for Robinhood Chain assets ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bitgo-stock-gains-after-adding-robinhood-chain-custody-support-93CH-4771591)); detailed custody arrangements for the underlying shares backing RHJ's debt securities are not fully disclosed publicly.
- **Private-company tokens** (OpenAI, SpaceX): wrapped exposure to special-purpose vehicles holding private shares — see §4 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/07/robinhood-stock-tokens-face-scrutiny-in-the-eu-after-openai-warning.html)).

## 4. Regulatory posture

- **EU framing:** Tokenized securities are **excluded from MiCA** and instead fall under **MiFID II and the Prospectus Regulation**. Classic Stock Tokens are MiFID II derivatives offered by Robinhood Europe, UAB (licensed brokerage + CASP under the **Bank of Lithuania**); the new Stock Tokens are debt securities under a prospectus regime via the Jersey issuer ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15254023-tokenized-equities-2026-backed-dinari-robinhood); [Robinhood EU](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/about-stock-tokens/)).
- **OpenAI/SpaceX controversy (July 2025):** Robinhood gave away OpenAI and SpaceX tokens at the EU launch. **OpenAI publicly disavowed them** ("these 'OpenAI tokens' are not OpenAI equity… We did not partner with Robinhood… and do not endorse it"), noting equity transfers require its approval. The **Bank of Lithuania opened a review**, seeking clarifications on the token structure and consumer communications. CEO Vlad Tenev downplayed the concerns, arguing the tokens give retail "exposure" via Robinhood's SPV stake ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/07/robinhood-stock-tokens-face-scrutiny-in-the-eu-after-openai-warning.html); [CNBC — Tenev response](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/08/robinhood-ceo-downplays-openai-concerns-on-tokenized-stock-structure.html); [Digital Watch](https://dig.watch/updates/lithuania-questions-legality-of-robinhoods-new-tokens)).
- **SEC engagement:** In April–May 2025 Robinhood submitted a **42-page proposal to the SEC Crypto Task Force** for a federal tokenized-RWA framework — a "Real World Asset Exchange" premised on **legal equivalence** (token = underlying asset's rights/treatment), uniform federal standards, and T+0 settlement ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/robinhood-sec-tokenized-rwa-framework-proposal); [CCN](https://www.ccn.com/news/crypto/robinhood-sec-federal-tokenized-rwa-framework/)).
- **Innovation exemption saga:** SEC Chair Paul Atkins previewed an "**innovation exemption**" sandbox (roughly 12–36 months, allowing tokenized US equities to trade without full registration, with 24/7 trading and fractionalization but excluding voting/dividend rights). The SEC **delayed the planned May 2026 rollout** after pushback from Nasdaq, NYSE, and Cboe on market-structure and surveillance risks; as of late May 2026 there was no new timeline ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/05/18/sec-to-propose-tokenized-stock-framework-as-wall-street-efforts-deepen-bloomberg); [Phemex recap](https://phemex.com/blogs/sec-delays-tokenized-stock-innovation-exemption-reasons); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/05/19/america-is-about-to-have-two-stock-markets-for-the-same-company/)). Consequently, **Stock Tokens remain unavailable to US persons** ([docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/)).
- **Industry opposition:** **SIFMA** and Citadel Securities oppose broad exemptive relief, arguing tokenized securities need the same investor protections (custody, broker-dealer separation, limits on vertical integration) and formal rulemaking rather than ad hoc exemptions ([SIFMA letter](https://www.sifma.org/advocacy/letters/request-for-exemptive-relief); [SIFMA blog](https://www.sifma.org/news/blog/tokenized-securities-markets-require-strong-investor-protections-not-broad-exemptive-relief); [US News/Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2025-10-08/crypto-race-to-tokenize-stocks-raises-investor-protection-flags)).
- Elsewhere: Singapore capital markets services licence from MAS; Canada launch via the ~$180M WonderFi acquisition (closed June 1, 2026); UK crypto launch pending, currently operating via Bitstamp ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)).

## 5. Fee model and economics

- **EU Classic Stock Tokens:** zero commission; only a **0.1% FX fee** each way (EUR↔USD conversion) ([Robinhood EU FAQ](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/stock-tokens-faq/)).
- **On-chain:** gas paid in ETH; at launch Robinhood is **covering gas for eligible users for 90 days** on swaps, crypto, Stock Token transactions, and bridging; Lighter perps zero-fee for 90 days with a 2x points multiplier (convertible to LIT; $11M committed by Lighter) ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)).
- **US crypto:** maker/taker fees "as low as 0%" for eligible professional traders; Canada zero commissions through Sept 30, 2026 ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/)).
- **Structural economics:** Galaxy Digital notes that by controlling the sequencer and the token issuance stack, Robinhood can **capture fees at every layer** (spread, trading fees, sequencer revenue) — analogous to Coinbase's Base, which Galaxy estimated earns >$150k/day in sequencer fees ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/robinhood-tokenization-nyse-liquidity-shift)).
- **Context:** the launch came amid headwinds — Q1 2026 crypto revenue down 47% YoY to $134M, crypto volumes down 48%, and a 10% workforce cut (~290 roles, ~$28M charges); HOOD rose ~5–8% on launch day ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/01/robinhood-rolls-out-public-blockchain-as-it-expands-deeper-into-crypto); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)).

## 6. Ecosystem

- **Day-one partners:** **Uniswap** (dedicated AMM as the primary public liquidity venue), **Pleiades** (proprietary AMM / prop trading venue), **Chainlink** (official oracle + cross-chain data), **BitGo** (custody), **Alchemy** (dev infra), **0x** (swap liquidity/cross-chain routing), **Morpho** (powers Robinhood Earn: USDG lending at ~7% est. APY, insured via Lloyd's of London/RELM), **Lighter** (perps DEX embedded in Robinhood Wallet), plus Ethena, Spark, Maple, Steakhouse, Rialto, Arcus, 1inch; testnet infra also included Allium, LayerZero, TRM, with Chainalysis adding automatic token support ([Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/robinhood-chain-launches-and-adopts-chainlink-to-unlock-access-to-the-onchain-economy-for-millions-of-users-302816242.html); [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/robinhood-chain-automatic-token-support/); [0x/TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/chainwire:145b55514094b:0-0x-to-power-swap-liquidity-and-cross-chain-access-on-robinhood-chain-at-launch/)).
- **Bitstamp:** acquired (deal closed June 2, 2025) to supply exchange licenses and institutional rails; Robinhood has discussed linking Bitstamp to its "tokenization engine" and uses it for UK operations ([Compliance Corylated](https://www.compliancecorylated.com/news/regulator-seeking-clarification-on-robinhoods-eu-stock-token-launch/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)).
- **Developer access — yes, open:** the chain is permissionless for contract deployment; public docs at [docs.robinhood.com/chain](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/); **$1M committed** to the 2026 Arbitrum Open House program (4 global buildathons, Founder Houses in NYC and London) ([Arbitrum blog](https://blog.arbitrum.io/robinhood-chain-testnet/)).
- **Bridges:** Arbitrum Bridge (testnet onboarding), LayerZero omnichain messaging, 0x cross-chain access, Chainlink cross-chain infrastructure ([Arbitrum blog](https://blog.arbitrum.io/robinhood-chain-testnet/); [docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/)).

## 7. Positioning and criticisms

**Positioning:** "Permissionless, AI-native, purpose-built for real-world assets" — Robinhood's bid to become the on-chain venue for tokenized TradFi, directly rivaling Coinbase's Base and positioning against traditional exchanges. Galaxy Digital argues the 24/7 tokenization push "directly challenges the deep concentration of liquidity… that gives major TradFi exchanges (e.g., NYSE) their competitive advantage," threatening exchange trading-fee and market-data revenue ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/robinhood-tokenization-nyse-liquidity-shift); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406918/robinhood-chain-goes-live-mainnet-alongside-24-7-tokenized-stocks-lighter-perps-planned-crypto-agentic-trading)).

**Criticisms:**

1. **"Not equity" caveat** — Stock Tokens are debt instruments/derivatives conferring no shareholder rights; critics say marketing blurs this distinction ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319564/20260702/robinhood-chain-goes-live-tokenized-stocks-key-ownership-caveat.htm); [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15254023-tokenized-equities-2026-backed-dinari-robinhood)).
2. **Private-company token controversy** — OpenAI's public disavowal and the Bank of Lithuania review remain the reputational low point ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/07/robinhood-stock-tokens-face-scrutiny-in-the-eu-after-openai-warning.html)).
3. **Centralization** — a single Robinhood-run sequencer (reportedly AWS-hosted with multisig control) raises censorship-resistance, liveness, and single-cloud-outage concerns; commentators question whether 100ms blocks justify the decentralization trade-off ([CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/02/robinhood-chain-claims-4x-solana-speed-with-100ms-blocks-but-do-we-really-need-ultra-fast-blockchains/)).
4. **Vertical-integration conflicts** — Robinhood issues the tokens, runs the sequencer, and operates the wallet/venue, capturing fees at every layer; SIFMA explicitly warns about vertical integration in tokenized markets ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/robinhood-tokenization-nyse-liquidity-shift); [SIFMA](https://www.sifma.org/news/blog/tokenized-securities-markets-require-strong-investor-protections-not-broad-exemptive-relief)).
5. **Liquidity fragmentation / two-tier markets** — off-hours token prices can dislocate from primary-market prices, and a parallel token market for the same company raises price-discovery and investor-protection questions ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/05/19/america-is-about-to-have-two-stock-markets-for-the-same-company/); [US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2025-10-08/crypto-race-to-tokenize-stocks-raises-investor-protection-flags)).
6. **US regulatory limbo** — with the SEC's innovation exemption delayed (May 2026, no new timeline), the flagship product is closed to Robinhood's home market ([Phemex](https://phemex.com/blogs/sec-delays-tokenized-stock-innovation-exemption-reasons)).

*Caveats: mainnet-launch details (partner roles, fee waivers, 100ms/"4x Solana" performance claims) largely trace to Robinhood's own announcements and launch-week press; the chain's data-availability mode and sequencer decentralization roadmap are not publicly documented and should be treated as unconfirmed.*
