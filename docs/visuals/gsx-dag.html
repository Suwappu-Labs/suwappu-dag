GSX DAG at a glance

Key facts

  • GSX DAG is the Layer 1 chain implementation.
  • Consensus is Mysticeti-C over a certificate DAG.
  • Execution is co-resident dual VM over the gsx-db substrate.
  • Security is split across Authority Ring and Validator Ring.
