Why provide compute here

The protocol is your customer

No marketplace bidding, no idle inventory. The network itself buys your work every epoch: certificate ordering, PQ attestations, shard storage, and data-availability serving.

Dollar-denominated pay

Rewards settle in a reserve-backed stablecoin. Your revenue does not ride a token chart — the design goal is boring, predictable infrastructure income.

Provably backed, or it doesn't pay

Every payout is minted only while an on-chain reserve-coverage proof shows the stablecoin is fully backed — including your payout. If coverage ever fails, payouts pause; they never debase.

No proof, no pay — for everyone

Rewards are computed from observed work: uptime probes, committed certificates, quorum signatures, SLA-checked serving. Nobody — including us — can pay a node that didn't do the work.

How the pay stays honest

GuaranteeMechanism
Proof-gated Zero passed storage audits, or zero answered uptime probes, earn zero. Pay scales with the audit pass ratio. Enforced in ltp/incentives.py and suwappu-precompiles::rewards.
Reserve-backed Reward mints route through the reserve-coverage circuit breaker and are checked at the post-mint supply. Coverage stale or short → the epoch's payout waits for a fresh attestation. Payouts can pause; they cannot be printed unbacked.
Budget-capped Each epoch has a hard payout ceiling. Oversubscribed epochs scale pro-rata — the schedule cannot run away.
Bond-secured Providers bond stablecoins (default posture $1,000). Failed audits slash the bond into the insurance pool on a progressive schedule; equivocation on the fast path forfeits 100%.

Become a provider testnet open

  1. Check hardware. A modern 8-core machine, 32 GB RAM, NVMe storage, and a stable 100 Mbps+ link is enough for the testnet. Signing keys are ML-DSA-65 (post-quantum) — generated by the node tooling, no HSM required until mainnet tiers.
  2. Sync the devnet. Follow DEVNET.md, point your node at rpc.devnet.suwappu.bot, and watch it on the explorer.
  3. Register for the points program. KYC (one operator, one validator), then your uptime and certificate work starts accruing weekly points on the public leaderboard.
  4. TGE conversion. Testnet points convert pro-rata into the 5–8% testnet allocation of mainnet supply.
  5. Mainnet epochs next — the same node, the same proofs, settling per-epoch in reserve-backed stablecoin under the mechanism above.
