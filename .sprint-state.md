**Liveness bug found while stabilising the restart test (pre-existing,
not S34's):** authors never voted for their own certificates, so a
certificate could gather at most `n − 1` Validator-Ring votes and a
four-node ring with one member down (3 × 150k = 450k of a 600k table,
threshold 400,001 — but the author's missing vote left 300k) held its
commit frontier until the member returned. This is the "three seeds
stalled while v3 was down" observation above; the block-availability rule
was not the cause. Fixed: the round driver records and broadcasts the
author's own vote. `restart_resumes_from_disk_without_equivocating` now
passes 5/5 with an outage of 2.5× `gc_depth`.

**Bug found by the restart test under a fully parallel suite (real, not
load):** a node counted checkpoint signatures only against its single
"pending" checkpoint, so a peer's signature for boundary B arriving after
the node had crossed B + cadence (it queues behind certificate frames in
the same FIFO inbox) was never counted. Under load the seeds stopped
co-signing at the first boundary after an outage, and the restarted node,
whose only served checkpoint was then below its own frontier, waited for a
snapshot forever. Signatures now aggregate against every recently emitted
checkpoint (`emitted_checkpoints`, bounded), and a co-signed height
settles everything at or below it.
