## Properties verified

1. **`seven_of_nine_attestation`** (LOAD-BEARING) — any 7-of-9 honest
   signatures over any payload produce a verifying aggregate under any
   of the C(9,7)=36 size-7 signer subsets.
2. **`six_of_nine_below_quorum`** — exactly 6 signatures yields
   `BelowQuorum { have: 6, need: 7 }`.
3. **`unknown_witness_rejected`** — a signature from an authority id
   outside the corridor is rejected with `UnknownWitness`.
4. **`payload_tamper_breaks_verification`** — flipping any byte of the
   payload after aggregation invalidates `verify_attestation` with
   `AggregateVerificationFailed`.
