2. **`forged_hop_breaks_chain`** — flipping any byte of any hop's
   MAC breaks verification, and the predicate identifies the precise
   hop index.

   `trc.as_keys` is rejected with `UnauthorizedAs`.

   strictly less than `now` is rejected with `HopExpired`, even when
   its MAC chain is honest.
