## Theorem 2 verified

The load-bearing safety property of the chain:

> A safety violation of the GSX DAG Layer 1 requires Byzantine corruption
> of *both* the Authority Ring and the Validator Ring quorums
> simultaneously.

Properties verified:

1. **`joint_quorum_safety`** (LOAD-BEARING) — every conflicting joint
   commit pair at the same round implies (Authority equivocators ≥
   ⌈n/3⌉) AND (Validator double-vote stake > total/3). Single-leg
   corruption cannot produce a conflict.
2. **`single_authority_corruption_safe`** — Authority equivocation
   alone, no Validator double-voting, never produces a conflicting joint
   commit.
3. **`single_validator_corruption_safe`** — Validator double-voting
   alone, no Authority equivocation, never produces a conflicting joint
   commit.
4. **`joint_commit_implies_authority_commit`** — joint commits are a
   strict subset of Authority commits; the joint rule tightens, never
   weakens.
