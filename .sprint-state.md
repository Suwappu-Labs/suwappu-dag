**Liveness bug found while stabilising the restart test (pre-existing,
not S34's):** authors never voted for their own certificates, so a
certificate could gather at most `n − 1` Validator-Ring votes and a
four-node ring with one member down (3 × 150k = 450k of a 600k table,
threshold 400,001 — but the author's missing vote left 300k) held its
commit frontier until the member returned. This is the "three seeds
stalled while v3 was down" observation above; the block-availability rule
was not the cause. Fixed: the round driver records and broadcasts the
author's own vote. `restart_resumes_from_disk_without_equivocating` now
passes 5/5 with an outage of 2.5× `gc_depth`.
